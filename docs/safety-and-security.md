# Safety and security

## Entrance security systems

Residents should not give entry via the remote control systems in the Blocks to anyone unknown to them, because, on occasions in the past, unauthorised persons have gained entry by asking for access over the intercom. Please remain vigilant and only allow access to your visitors to maintain the safety of the building.

## Fire alarms

!!! important "Updated guidance in the August 2026 booklet"
    The previous booklet's instruction to evacuate to the main car park has been superseded. The [August 2026 information booklet (printed pages 3-4)](assets/bishops-wharf-leaflet.pdf) states that the Fire Risk Assessor has recommended a Stay-Put Policy. It says property owners have been sent a separate explanatory leaflet by the Managing Agent. Refer to that leaflet for how the policy operates, and contact the Managing Agent if you need a copy or clarification.

The fire alarm control panels are in the ground floor communal areas of the blocks. Contact details for the fire alarm maintenance company are displayed on the notice board.

The current booklet states that property owners are responsible for complying with the recommendations in their latest Fire Risk Assessment relating to works or requirements within their property, using suitable materials and qualified professionals.

If anyone is made aware of a fire in a block, or when an automatic fire alarm is activated, the booklet says they should immediately dial 999 and request attendance from the fire brigade.
